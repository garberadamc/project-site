Data source:

In this lab we use an open source data set called General Social Survey (GSS). It contains responses from U.S. participants from 2000–2014 including like age, marital status, political party, religion, income, and hours of TV watched. These are real survey responses!

Qn: What kind of data is gss_raw and how many rows and columns does it have?

Response: gss_raw is a tibble (data frame) with 21,483 rows and 9 columns, containing the GSS survey data from 2000–2014. Each row is one person’s responses, and the columns include year of survey, age, marital status, political party, religion, income, party ID, religion, denomination, and number of TV hours watched per day.
